The site uses the CEDICT Chinese-English dictionary to provide pinyin and dictionary entries for the Chinese terms. I picked CEDICT because it  is extensive and licensed under the Creative Commons. But it is primarily a modern Chinese dictionary, so the entries it displays may not map onto the meaning of these terms in the *Mengzi*. For example, CEDICT renders 中國 as "China," but in pre-Qin texts this refers to several distinct political entities, and is better translated as "the central states." (see Rouzer p. 258)
